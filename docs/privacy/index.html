Privacy Policy

ShotTraxx™

Last updated: September 25, 2026

ShotTraxx™ is a golf app that marks shots with your phone’s GPS, keeps a scorecard, and shows yards to the green. This page describes what the app does with information. There is no ShotTraxx account and no sign-in.

Rounds stay on your phone

Rounds, scores, clubs, and shot marks are stored on the device. You can export a copy of your rounds or restore from a file you choose. We do not sell personal information.

Location, while the app is open

ShotTraxx uses location only while you are using the app (When In Use). It does not use Always location, and it does not collect location in the background.

While the app is open, that location is used to:

  • find courses near you
  • show yards to the green
  • mark where you hit from when you pick a club

The Apple Watch companion can use the Watch’s location when you pick a club on the Watch, for that same shot mark. Nearby course search stays on the phone.

A nearby-course search sends that location to our server (the share Worker) so a course provider can return courses around you. Hole-map outlines can also be requested from OpenStreetMap around the course or green. The hole map on iPhone uses Apple Maps. Those requests are for the course list and the map. They are not a location profile that we sell.

Camera and photos

Camera and photo library are optional. They are used only when you add a course from a paper scorecard.

  • Camera: take a photo of that scorecard.
  • Photo library: read the one photo you pick.

ShotTraxx does not save photos to your library. The photo is attached to an email that you send. The app does not upload that photo on its own.

Sharing a round

Sharing is optional. If you share a live board or a scorecard, the app can store that scorecard on the share Worker so someone with the code or link can open it.

What is sent is the scorecard: course name, holes, par, scores, putts, club and yards, and start and finish times. It does not include GPS coordinates or a shot trail.

The share sheet can also send a scorecard image through an app you pick, such as Messages or Mail. That goes to whoever you choose.

If you help add a course, the email you send can include the course name, city, the email you type, hole pins you saved on the course, and the scorecard photo. You tap Send. Nothing is mailed until you do.

We do not sell this information.

Course maps

Course and map data, such as tees and greens, may be fetched through the share Worker. The keys for those course providers stay on the Worker. They are not in the app.

A copy of that course geometry may be kept on the phone and, when a shared cache is configured, on that cache, so the same course does not have to be fetched again. The cache is course data, not your round.

Apple Watch

The Watch app is a companion to the round on your phone. It uses an Apple App Group on your devices, together with Watch Connectivity, to show the same hole, clubs, yards to the green, and putts. It does not collect extra kinds of information beyond that round, and it does not have its own account.

During a round the Watch starts a golf workout session so the app can stay up. ShotTraxx does not read Health data, does not save that session to Health, and does not send Health data off the Watch.

Children

ShotTraxx is a golf app for players. It is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.
